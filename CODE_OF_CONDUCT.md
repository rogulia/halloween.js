# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in halloween.js — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in feedback, especially in code review.
- Assume good faith; disagreements about code or direction are normal and fine.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution for reasons unrelated to its technical quality (project direction, scope, aesthetic fit).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at **iurii@rogulia.fi**. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
